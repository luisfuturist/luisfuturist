# Emidio

Curiosity pulled me from videogames to category theory — wondering how things worked turned into programming, then into the formal side: linguistics, programming languages, logic, and math. That intersection is where my work lives now, across tools, agents, and research.

Lately I'm drawn to orchestrating AI agents — not just as product features, but as participants in the development process itself — and to treating everything as code: an executable, reproducible path from idea to deployment. I graduated with a degree in AI from [FIAP](https://github.com/fiap), though most of what I know I learned independently, by hobby and by curiosity.

Philosophy and the future keep me thinking; cyberpunk, my favorite genre, keeps me thinking and imagining, which drives me even further for answers and experience seeking.
